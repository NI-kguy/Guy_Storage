Robataya Izakaya

Japanese Food Avg: RM 30 – RM 80 / pax

Robataya Izakaya is a Japanese casual dining restaurant that brings together two beloved Japanese traditions — Robatayaki (炉端焼き, charcoal fireside cooking) and Izakaya (居酒屋, a relaxed Japanese pub). Located in Penang's Queenbay area, it offers an authentic Japanese dining experience where food is grilled slowly over binchōtan charcoal and shared among diners in a warm, communal atmosphere — evoking the spirit of a traditional Japanese gathering around the hearth.

🔥 What is Robatayaki?

Robatayaki (炉端焼き) literally means "fireside cooking". This centuries-old method originates from the fishermen of Hokkaido, Japan's northernmost island, who cooked over a communal open hearth called an irori on their boats using binchōtan (white charcoal) encased in stone boxes. The technique uses a wide, flat open fireplace, cooking food at varying speeds to lock in natural flavours. By 1965, there were over 10,000 robata restaurants across Japan.

🍺 What is an Izakaya?

An Izakaya (居酒屋) is a type of informal Japanese bar and dining establishment — the word literally translates to "sit-down sake shop". Dating back to Japan's Edo period (1603–1867), izakayas are the Japanese equivalent of a pub or tavern, where people relax after work over food and drinks. Dishes are shared tapas-style, ordered gradually throughout the session. Diners receive a warm oshibori (towel) and a small starter (otōshi) upon arrival.

🏠 Dining Experience

  • Guests are seated around a central open charcoal grill
  • Fresh ingredients are displayed — point to order what you want grilled
  • Food is served progressively: light starters → grilled mains → rice/noodles
  • Dishes are shared among the table, similar to Spanish tapas
  • Nomi-hōdai (all-you-can-drink) options may be available
  • Ideal for group dinners, celebrations, and after-work gatherings

🌟 Atmosphere & Highlights

  • Warm, rustic Japanese interior inspired by traditional irori hearths
  • Smoky, aromatic ambiance from live charcoal grilling
  • Authentic binchōtan charcoal for clean, intense heat
  • Japanese beverages: sake, shōchū, beer, chuhai cocktails
  • Relaxed, unhurried pace — great for long, leisurely meals
  • Suitable for both couples and larger groups

Madam Kwan's

Malaysian Food Avg: RM 25 – RM 60 / pax

A well-established Malaysian restaurant chain celebrated for its authentic local cuisine in a comfortable, casual setting. Known for its signature Nasi Bojari (a grand mixed-rice platter) and traditional Malay-Chinese fusion dishes. A favourite among both locals and tourists looking for a true taste of Malaysia.

Dandy

Western / Modern Casual Avg: RM 20 – RM 50 / pax

A stylish modern casual dining restaurant with a relaxed, café-like ambiance. Popular among younger diners, Dandy offers a creative menu of Western comfort food with some local influences — think hearty burgers, artisan pastas, all-day brunch, and indulgent desserts. Great for casual hangouts and weekend brunches.
